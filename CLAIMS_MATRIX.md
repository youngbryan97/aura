# Aura claims and evidence

The table records what each result supports. A local demonstration covers
its named task, setup, and run. To check whether a feature is serving on a
particular Mac, consult that installation's configuration and records.

Retracted results retain their data and carry a retraction notice. The
numbered test procedures and failure conditions follow the summary.
