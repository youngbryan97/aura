The ring

Three recordings, and twice the answer was about the recording

Every design document draws the same loop: interoception, affect, the global workspace, higher-order monitoring, the self model, planning, action, back to interoception. The test returned does not close three times.

The first two recordings drove each station by calling its readers — a getter, a status, a snapshot. Calling a getter on affect hands nothing to the workspace, so no station could influence another, and a recording in which influence is impossible cannot show influence. The stations are coupled by the state object the kernel's 29-phase pipeline passes from one to the next, and that object only moves while a turn is being taken.

Then the station table turned out to be written from module names, and the modules named after the stations do not run a turn. None of the 129 workspace cells and none of the 272 action cells fired in 240 turns. Reporting that as an architecture is reporting a dictionary.

Then the turns turned out to be system ticks, on which the response phase produces no reply. The action station never acted, and the one link that measured at chance was the one out of it.

Driving user-facing turns instead — every condition but the idle tick answers, at 151 to 193 characters — all seven links carry, in all six conditions. Action back to interoception included: median gain +0.0033 over its own rotations, better than them on 88.6% of 493 pairs.

Which sounds like the ring closing, and is not.

Where the ring sits among the other thirty-five

Seven links that all carry mean one thing when nothing else does and another when everything does. Measuring every ordered pair of stations rather than only the seven: 42 of 42 carry. A pipeline over one shared state object couples every stage to every other by construction, so a link carrying separates nothing, and reporting the seven without that denominator would read as evidence for a ring.

The ring's seven come 4th, 5th, 8th, 12th, 24th, 30th and 37th of 42 by strength. Scored against all 720 directed cycles through the same stations — exactly, not by sampling — the architecture's order comes out stronger than 25% to 55% of them depending on the condition, at z −0.65 to −1.03.

What the measurement supports is not the ring. It is that the self model is the hub: what she holds about herself says more about her interoceptive and affective state than anything in the system says about anything else.

The instrument

Two measurements that looked right

The first scored every cross-station pair with an F test and a false-discovery correction. All seven links carried, at 93 to 100% of pairs. Then a sample was re-run with the source rotated in time, and a quarter of the rotated pairs passed the same threshold. A parametric p-value on a spike train sampled every two milliseconds is not a p-value.

The second put the population's recent past into both models, on the theory that what the test had found was the shared rhythm of a turn. It barely moved — the tell for a periodic workload. A turn repeats, so rotating a source by any shift lands it on another turn and preserves the alignment the null was meant to destroy.

So rotation became the null rather than a check on one. Each pair is scored against its own eight rotations and the link on the median paired difference, with a bootstrap interval over pairs. That separates: ring links score 0.006 to 0.101, and station pairs outside the ring score 0.0002 to 0.004.

The cut

do(station = 0), against a lesion of the same size somewhere else

Everything above measures what varies with what. A lesion does the other thing: replace a phase's execute with one that returns nothing, run the same six objectives again, put it back. Removing anything from a running system changes something downstream, so every cut is scored against a control cut of the same number of phases at the closest connectivity.

Five defects in the experiment, each found by running it. The first readouts were coherence_score, phi_estimate, last_response and active_goals — every one of them constant across all six objectives and both affect arms with the model held fixed. All four would have read no change under every lesion, and all three predictions would have come out confirmed with no measurement behind any of them. A readout is now refused unless it moved at baseline.

One kernel served every arm in sequence, so a drift down the run read as an effect of the cut. version counts writes to the state, so silencing any writing phase decrements it by construction. hash(mode) was a readout, in a codebase already bitten by hash randomisation. And “refuted” was reported for a readout the control moved further — which is the wrong readout, not a refutation.

The last row is the clearest thing here. Push her valence 1.2 apart between two runs and by the end of a turn the difference is 0.0006 — the affect phases have removed 99.95% of it. Cut those two phases and the whole 1.2 survives. They are not decoration on the pipeline; they are a controller.

Of the three predictions written from the theory, one was partly confirmed and two were not attributable — their readouts moved further under the control. What the first run found was registered as three more predictions and run again on an independent seed. All three confirmed.

The mesh

The code injected into one end and read the other

Everything above is made of functions. The neural mesh is not: 64 cortical columns of 64 neurons, wired by a distance-decayed weight matrix, running at 10 Hz on its own clock. Keeping it in a separate object made one question unanswerable, because a distance needs both ends in the same graph.

Putting the columns in — they are the red band in the plate above — made it askable. Two functions drive the mesh and five read it. Can what is injected reach what is read? Across eight seeds, nothing injected into the sensory tier could reach a single executive column. Not once.

One missing pathway caused it. The mesh builds its top-down feedback explicitly, tier pair by tier pair, and left the bottom-up direction to the local wiring — whose probability decays as exp(−|i − j| × 0.15). A tier boundary is exactly where that index distance is largest: sensory column 0 to executive column 48 works out at 0.05 × e^−7.2, about one edge in twenty-eight thousand. The 1.5× feedforward bias inside that function could only ever have applied to edges the decay had already made impossible.

The fix builds the bottom-up pathway the way the top-down one is built and leaves distance out of it, because a projection between cortical areas is an axon bundle and its existence does not fall off with how far apart the areas are. The density is the same 0.05 the local wiring already uses. 520 consciousness and mesh tests still pass.
