# Aura glossary: terms in ordinary language

Status: Generated reference · Definitions reviewed 2026-10-25

Use this page when a term stops you understanding a sentence. The context matters: the same word or letter can have different meanings in different experiments.

For arithmetic, read [the formula guide](FORMULAS.md). For the overall idea, read [the plain-English tour](PLAIN_ENGLISH_GUIDE.md). [Scientific sources](SCIENCE_AND_SOURCES.md) explains the research connections; [current ISC status](ISC_STATUS.md) gives results rather than definitions.

**168 contextual definitions**, sorted alphabetically. Examples explain the term; they are not extra measured results.

| Term and context | Meaning | Why it matters in practice | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ablation](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#measurement) (Tests, controls and claims) | Disable a component for comparison | Asks whether that component matters to the outcome | A quality loss after removal can support a bounded causal claim |
| [Action](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#browser) (Using a browser or screen) | A click, key or other interface operation | Attempts a state change | A dispatched action can fail to affect the intended object |
| [Activation](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#models) (Language models and internal steering) | A value computed inside a forward pass | Can carry an intervention into generation | A change at one layer may have a different effect from the same change elsewhere |
| [Anchor](ISC_VARIABLES.md) (ISC) | A saved comparison moment from which experimental arms begin. | Accepted anchors, collected anchors and independent observations are different counts. | A collected moment can be unusable if one required arm is unreadable. |
| [Argmax](MATHEMATICAL_CLAIM_LIMITS.md) (Mathematical claim limits) | The choice whose score is greatest among the candidates considered. | Scores can change without changing the winner. | Scores (10, 1) and (9, 2) choose the first action both times. |
| [Arm](ISC_EXPLAINED.md) (Experiment) | One treatment or control path in a comparison. | Arms must share the intended starting conditions without sharing later residue. | One copy receives an intervention; another is left unchanged. |
| [Arousal](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#affect) (Affect and operating condition) | An internal activation or intensity coordinate | Can change the strength or urgency of processing | It is separate from whether a condition is positive or negative |
| [Artifact](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#observability) (Logs, measurements and evidence trails) | A retained output used as evidence | Allows later inspection or reproduction | A filename does not prove that the file contains measured data |
| [Assumption](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#engineering) (Engineering calculations and drawings) | A condition taken as given | Defines where the result applies | Changing the load case can invalidate the result |
| [Authority](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#governance) (Permission, actions and receipts) | The rule or actor entitled to make a decision | Defines who can permit a consequence | A generated plan cannot grant itself new authority |
| [Autocorrelation](FORMULAS.md#trend-and-time-to-a-threshold) (Statistics) | A signal resembles its own earlier values. | Closely related samples can carry less independent information than their count suggests. | Memory usage in adjacent seconds is often similar. |
| [Backpressure](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#resources) (Memory, speed and resource limits) | A request to slow or yield when a consumer cannot keep up | Limits queues and competing work | An expected yield should not automatically be reported as a dead system |
| [Baseline](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#affect) (Affect and operating condition) | A recorded reference level | Makes a change interpretable relative to ordinary state | Above usual and below usual need an actual reference |
| [Baseline](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#physical) (Claims about the physical world) | The comparison without the proposed effect | Separates the result from ordinary variation | A warm room can heat a sensor without the device’s contribution |
| [Bootstrap](ISC_VARIABLES.md) (Statistics) | Recalculate a statistic on resampled data to estimate variability. | The resampling unit and dependence assumptions affect the interval. | Resample the declared comparison units rather than treating every time frame as independent. |
| [Calibration](SCIENCE_AND_SOURCES.md#measurement-and-statistical-interpretation) (Measurement) | Check whether scores or forecasts match observed outcomes under stated conditions. | A confident number needs evidence that its interpretation is justified. | Score a resource forecast when its deadline arrives. |
| [Candidate](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#attention) (Attention and competing concerns) | A proposed item for attention | Enters a competition | A memory and an intention can have different content types |
| [Capability](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#governance) (Permission, actions and receipts) | A scoped authorisation or declared ability, depending on context | Must be read with its scope | A token for one file is not unrestricted file access |
| [Carrier](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#isc) (ISC and the subjecthood research) | The process proposed to carry the state being studied | Must be identified by the carrier experiment | A name or a passing synergy statistic does not select it |
| [Causal claim](ISC_EXPLAINED.md) (Experiment) | A claim about what changes because a factor is changed. | A correlation or a module name alone does not supply that comparison. | Changing internal valence changes the measured response under controls. |
| [Causal closure](FORMULAS.md#closure-leak) (ISC) | The proposed boundary is adequate under the declared outside-information test and margin. | It is conditional on measurement coverage, estimator and environment. | An outside driver may improve prediction and keep the boundary open. |
| [Certificate](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#models) (Language models and internal steering) | A record of a measured basis under a stated protocol | Binds a channel claim to its tested conditions | A channel certificate can pass while installation is refused |
| [Channel](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#physical) (Claims about the physical world) | The route intended to produce an effect | Connects a request with a possible action | A declared channel can still be unavailable |
| [Checkpoint](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#documentation) (Reading records and research plans) | A recorded stage of work or source history | Makes a change recoverable and reviewable | A commit is not a claim that every goal is complete |
| [Checkpoint](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#reliability) (Staying alive and recovering) | Saved state for continuing work | Can limit lost progress after interruption | Its presence does not prove that restoration has been tested |
| [Closure](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#isc) (ISC and the subjecthood research) | Whether the proposed boundary omits an important internal driver after declared input is accounted for | Can remain unresolved rather than pass by default | K* now uses a declared numeric margin about 0.0196 plus an interventional closure test; the earlier least-domain rule stays withdrawn |
| [Confidence interval](ISC_VARIABLES.md) (Statistics) | An interval produced by a procedure designed for stated repeated-sampling coverage. | Its interpretation depends on the procedure and assumptions. | An upper bound above the declared margin cannot establish closure within that margin. |
| [Configuration](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#getting-started) (Getting started) | Settings read by the program | Only a setting with a reader can change behavior | An invented environment variable changes nothing |
| [Conjunction](FORMULAS.md#j-star-and-the-bridge-are-structured-claims) (Decision rule) | Every required condition must hold. | A strong result on one condition does not replace a failed required condition. | Four bridge grounds do not replace an unresolved fifth. |
| [Context window](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#models) (Language models and internal steering) | The token budget available to a model request | Limits how much text fits at once | A memory store can be larger than the material placed in one request |
| [Contraction](MATHEMATICAL_CLAIM_LIMITS.md) (Mathematical claim limits) | An update that shrinks distances by a factor strictly below one on a declared domain. | A near-one cosine between two states does not establish contraction. | F(x)=2x preserves direction but doubles distances. |
| [Control](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#measurement) (Tests, controls and claims) | A comparison that reveals another explanation | Can expose effects of the measuring procedure | An unchanged copy should not look like a deliberate intervention |
| [Control delay](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#vision) (Visual control and moving worlds) | Time between choosing an action and its effect | Can change whether a plan succeeds | A correct action chosen too late may miss |
| [Core](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#isc) (ISC and the subjecthood research) | The selected group of measured state | Defines the proposed process boundary | Useful internal causes outside it can make the description incomplete |
| [Counterfactual](ISC_EXPLAINED.md) (Causal reasoning) | What would happen under an alternative stated intervention or condition. | It makes a dependence claim testable. | What would the target do if this connection were removed? |
| [Coverage](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#measurement) (Tests, controls and claims) | What cases or paths were actually checked | Limits generalisation | An offline unit test does not cover a production microphone |
| [Credential](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#security) (Privacy and trust boundaries) | A secret or token used to authenticate access | Must be handled within its authorised scope | A token name in documentation is not the secret value |
| [Delta, Δ](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | A change or configured intervention | Shows a difference rather than an absolute level | Going from 10 to 13 gives a change of +3 |
| [Denotation](MATHEMATICAL_CLAIM_LIMITS.md) (Mathematical claim limits) | The mathematical function or meaning assigned to a program under specified semantics. | A shorter name can preserve meaning while changing search cost. | Naming a long calculation does not add a new computable function. |
| [Dependency](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#architecture) (Architecture and runtime components) | A component or value another part relies on | Explains what must be available | A broken dependency can cause a fallback rather than the intended mechanism |
| [Depth](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#reasoning) (Reasoning and checking answers) | Additional stages or repeated computation | Trades time and resources for a possible reasoning benefit | A deeper pass still needs a measured correct-answer gain |
| [Drive](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#affect) (Affect and operating condition) | A represented need or motivation | Contributes to proposed work or choices | A curiosity value should change a relevant choice to support a causal claim |
| [Effect receipt](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#physical) (Claims about the physical world) | A record of independent post-action measurement | Supports a scoped outcome claim | It is stronger than a transport acknowledgement |
| [Effect size](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#measurement) (Tests, controls and claims) | How much a measured quantity changed | Shows practical magnitude | Statistical significance alone does not state a useful improvement |
| [Embedding](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#memory) (Memory and recall) | A numerical representation used for similarity search | Helps find related material | Similar wording and similar meaning can produce different retrieval behavior |
| [Epsilon lower bound](MATHEMATICAL_CLAIM_LIMITS.md) (Mathematical claim limits) | A positive floor on success probability for each eligible repeated trial in a stated argument. | Some positive chance at each time is weaker than a common positive floor. | With a common floor e, missing after n trials has bound (1-e)^n. |
| [Epsilon or margin](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | A declared acceptable residual or error bound, depending on context | Defines the precision of a claim | An unnamed margin cannot be inferred from a convenient observed result |
| [Expected value](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#learning) (Learning and kept procedures) | Expected benefit minus relevant cost | Can decide whether a retained rule pays for itself | A rule that saves 5 units but costs 7 is not beneficial on that accounting |
| [Expected value, EV](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | Expected benefit minus the relevant costs under a stated accounting | Helps decide whether a retained method is worth using | Benefit 5 and cost 7 give net value -2 |
| [Experimental basis](DEMOS_AND_RELATED_WORK.md#steering) (Steering) | A measured direction or set of directions used for an intervention. | A basis used in an experiment is not automatically authorised for desktop serving. | A channel certificate can pass while an installation qualification fails. |
| [Extensional property](MATHEMATICAL_CLAIM_LIMITS.md) (Mathematical claim limits) | A property of what a program computes, rather than how its code is written. | Rice’s theorem needs this and other assumptions; it does not prohibit every program proof. | Two differently spelled programs can compute the same function. |
| [Fail closed](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#governance) (Permission, actions and receipts) | Refuse or withhold when required evidence is absent | Prevents an unknown from silently becoming permission | A missing verifier is not a passing verifier |
| [False positive](FORMULAS.md#finite-randomisation-p-values) (Measurement) | The procedure declares an effect when the relevant null condition holds. | Nulls and decision levels need a declared interpretation. | A noisy unchanged copy crosses the same threshold as a cut. |
| [Falsifiable](RESEARCH_GUIDE.md) (Research) | The claim has a specified possible observation that would count against it. | A test that always passes cannot discriminate the hypothesis. | Declare the failure condition before measuring. |
| [Feedback](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#browser) (Using a browser or screen) | The observed consequence | Checks whether the action worked | A changed page is evidence; a tool returning success is only one signal |
| [Finding](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#engineering) (Engineering calculations and drawings) | A calculated result carrying its derivation | Connects a drawing to evidence | A dimension should be traceable to its formula and inputs |
| [Fingerprint or digest](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | A hash of specified data or configuration | Detects that an input or file changed | Two different campaign fingerprints must not silently become one result |
| [Free energy](TERMINOLOGY.md) (Model-dependent term) | A quantity defined by a particular inference model; its meaning needs the model and equation. | It is not automatically thermodynamic energy or a measure of felt discomfort. | Read the named implementation rather than inferring joules from the label. |
| [Functional evidence](ISC_EXPLAINED.md) (Evidence scope) | Evidence about computations, dependencies or behavior under specified tests. | It supports that mechanism without settling every interpretation of experience. | A state change reaches recall through a measured route. |
| [GB and GiB](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#resources) (Memory, speed and resource limits) | Decimal and binary memory units: 1 GB is 1,000,000,000 bytes; 1 GiB is 1,073,741,824 bytes | Different tools can describe the same capacity with different numbers | 64 GiB is about 68.7 GB; it is not extra newly available memory |
| [Generated document](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#documentation) (Reading records and research plans) | A page rendered from another source | Must be updated through its renderer | Hand editing can be overwritten or detach it from its evidence |
| [Goal](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#agency) (Goals and autonomous work) | A represented outcome to pursue | Organises further work | Completing a substep is different from completing the goal |
| [Guide](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#documentation) (Reading records and research plans) | An explanation intended to describe current use or concepts | Provides an entry point | Read its scope and current status links |
| [Hash](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#observability) (Logs, measurements and evidence trails) | A fingerprint of specified bytes | Detects a changed file relative to a recorded digest | It does not establish that the original contents were correct |
| [Held state](ISC_VARIABLES.md) (Current ISC method) | Declared attributes counted on the core side of the K* closure test. | They must not also be treated as outside predictors. | Readings under held. move with the domain they serve during a cut. |
| [Held-out task](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#reasoning) (Reasoning and checking answers) | A task excluded from fitting or method selection | Checks transfer beyond the development examples | Success on a new task is stronger than replaying a fitted example |
| [Historical record](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#documentation) (Reading records and research plans) | A dated account of a run or decision | Preserves provenance | A later failure or success should be a new record |
| [Hypothesis](phenomenology/README.md) (Research) | A proposed explanation with stated predictions and scope. | The comparison must distinguish it from an alternative. | The internal state matters versus the state is unused commentary. |
| [Identity continuity](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#vision) (Visual control and moving worlds) | Evidence that observations refer to the same object | Prevents tracking a distractor | Motion alone does not prove which object the agent controls |
| [IIT](SCIENCE_AND_SOURCES.md#consciousness-research-and-the-isc-argument) (Theory) | Integrated information theory: a specific formal account of intrinsic causal structure and experience. | A similarly named graph score is not automatically the theory's full calculation. | A Gaussian integration estimate needs its own estimator label. |
| [Impasse](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#attention) (Attention and competing concerns) | A decision that lacks a justified winner | Creates a reason for further work | An exact tie should not be disguised as a meaningful preference |
| [Initiative](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#agency) (Goals and autonomous work) | A proposed action or task | Can enter selection or governance | A proposal can be refused or deferred |
| [Input](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#engineering) (Engineering calculations and drawings) | A supplied quantity with a defined meaning and unit | Feeds the calculation | A length in metres cannot silently become millimetres |
| [Intervention](ISC_VARIABLES.md) (Experiment) | A deliberate, declared change to a measured system. | Its writer, size, duration and consumers determine what was tested. | Change one source domain while keeping the other declared conditions fixed. |
| [Interventional closure](ISC_VARIABLES.md) (Current ISC method) | The specified outside-state swaps leave the measured core and held state unchanged at the tested anchors and horizon. | It checks actual effects beyond the observational prediction comparison. | The current rule checks bit-for-bit equality within two turns when untouched twins agree. |
| [Invariant](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#architecture) (Architecture and runtime components) | A condition required to stay true | Can identify the stage that broke an assumption | A checker that fails to run is not evidence the condition held |
| [J-star](FORMULAS.md#j-star-and-the-bridge-are-structured-claims) (ISC bridge) | The combined description of carrier, content relations and causal lineage. | It is a structured identification, not a scalar score to maximise. | No carrier means the other recorded grounds cannot manufacture J-star. |
| [K*](ISC_VARIABLES.md) (Current ISC method) | The proposed core K plus declared held-state dependencies its transition reads. | It changes which readings count as inside the candidate boundary. | An identified history buffer is included alongside the original ten-domain vector. |
| [KV cache](../MODEL_CARD.md) (Model runtime) | Stored attention keys and values reused during generation. | Cache ownership, context size and invalidation affect memory and correctness. | A longer conversation can use more cache memory even with fixed model weights. |
| [Lambda](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | A context-dependent coefficient or residual-control symbol | Must be read from the local definition | The same letter can mean a decay setting in one model and an outside-control quantity in another |
| [Latency](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#resources) (Memory, speed and resource limits) | Elapsed time for a defined step | Measures delay experienced by a user or pipeline | State the start and end points, not just one undifferentiated speed number |
| [Latent state](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#reasoning) (Reasoning and checking answers) | Internal numerical representation rather than generated words | Can carry computation without printing each step | A latent operation needs evidence that its output affects the answer |
| [Likelihood ratio](FORMULAS.md#likelihood-ratios-and-assumptions) (Probability) | The evidence probability under one hypothesis divided by that under another. | A project score is not a calibrated likelihood ratio merely because it has that label. | Identical stipulated observation distributions give ratio one. |
| [Liveness](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#reliability) (Staying alive and recovering) | Whether a component is still making the required kind of progress | Helps distinguish stalled from merely slow | A heartbeat alone may not show that a request is advancing |
| [Log](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#observability) (Logs, measurements and evidence trails) | A recorded event or message | Retains context for diagnosis | A message saying success is weaker than a verified outcome |
| [Logit](FORMULAS.md#sampling-temperature) (Language model) | A model score before conversion into a token probability. | A logit is not already a probability. | Temperature rescales relative logits before sampling. |
| [Matrix, often W](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | A table of numbers used in a transformation | Can map one numerical representation to another | In the ISC domain labels, W instead means world model |
| [Mean](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | The arithmetic average | Summarises a collection while hiding variation between its entries | Changes +1 and +5 have mean +3 |
| [Metric](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#observability) (Logs, measurements and evidence trails) | A declared numerical measurement | Supports comparison over time | Its unit, scope and missing-value behavior matter |
| [MIP](FORMULAS.md#gaussian-integration-score) (Integration search) | Minimum-information partition under a specified objective and search. | A candidate cut and a certified minimum are different claims. | Exhaustive search can certify the small-grain minimum. |
| [Model lane](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#getting-started) (Getting started) | A route to a particular model or model service | The route can change resource needs and what answers a request | A smaller configured lane may answer while a larger one is unavailable |
| [Momentum](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#affect) (Affect and operating condition) | How a state carries forward while new input arrives | Controls persistence and settling | A value overwritten immediately cannot carry a sustained intervention |
| [Non-inferiority](MATHEMATICAL_CLAIM_LIMITS.md) (Mathematical claim limits) | A comparison asking whether a treatment is no worse than a baseline by more than a predeclared acceptable margin. | Overlapping confidence intervals do not establish it. | A declared loss margin needs an appropriate comparison, not a visual overlap. |
| [Norm](FORMULAS.md#vector-length-and-whitening) (Mathematics) | A declared way to measure the size of a vector. | Different coordinate scales can change an unnormalised distance. | The Euclidean norm of (3, 4) is 5. |
| [Null](FORMULAS.md#finite-randomisation-p-values) (Experiment) | The specified comparison model or condition representing the alternative being tested. | There is no universal null that rules out every explanation. | A shuffled comparison destroys a selected relationship while preserving others. |
| [Observable](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#physical) (Claims about the physical world) | The quantity to be measured | Defines what success would change | Temperature requires a declared sensor and unit |
| [Observation](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#browser) (Using a browser or screen) | The interface information actually read | Constrains what the agent can infer | An old screenshot may no longer describe the page |
| [Observational closure](ISC_VARIABLES.md) (Current ISC method) | The outside predictor adds no more than the permitted contribution under the specified estimator and controls. | A bounded predictive result needs a margin, uncertainty and measurement validity. | The upper bound must clear the named margin; a failed estimator fold cannot support closure. |
| [Outcome](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#agency) (Goals and autonomous work) | What was observed after acting | Feeds completion and learning decisions | A successful tool call need not mean the real task succeeded |
| [p value](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | The extremeness of a statistic under the declared null comparison | Belongs to one test and its assumptions | It is not the probability of consciousness |
| [P-value](FORMULAS.md#finite-randomisation-p-values) (Statistics) | How extreme the statistic is under the declared null procedure and assumptions. | It is not the probability of consciousness or the probability that the hypothesis is false. | Zero exceedances among finite random draws still does not produce p = 0. |
| [Parameter count, such as 27B](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#models) (Language models and internal steering) | The approximate number of stored model parameters; B means billion | Describes model size, not an intelligence or consciousness score | A 27B model can have different memory needs under different numeric formats |
| [Parity](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#isc) (ISC and the subjecthood research) | The project’s five-ground bridge conjunction under named assumptions | Organises supporting evidence | It is not an independently calibrated probability of experience |

| [Phi](VARIABLES_EXPLAINED.md#numbers) (Reading mathematical symbols) | A name used for several integration-related measurements | Its formula, state description and controls determine its meaning | Different phi implementations are not interchangeable consciousness meters |
