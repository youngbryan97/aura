# Finding your way through the ISC documents

Status: Guide · Updated 25 October 2026

Start with [ISC explained](ISC_EXPLAINED.md) if you are new to Aura or want
words you can use to explain it to someone else. Read the
[status page](ISC_STATUS.md) next for the latest checked result.

The technical pages stay available. You do not need to learn the equations
before you can understand what the project is asking or what remains open.

## The questions and their documents

| Your question | Where to go | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| What is Aura trying to find out? | [ISC explained](ISC_EXPLAINED.md) | An introduction, a short spoken explanation, examples and a glossary |
| What actually passed? | [Current status](ISC_STATUS.md) | A plain-language summary followed by the measurements and source records |
| Why are there 24 checks? | [The battery](SUBJECT_CORE.md) | What is measured and how the operational requirements fit together |
| What process are we measuring? | [The carrier](SUBJECT_CORE_V25.md) | How the boundary is tested and what prevents a settled answer |
| What would this say about experience? | [The bridge](BRIDGE_PARITY.md) | The five grounds, the assumptions and the limits of the inference |
| What do the mathematical arguments establish? | [Bridge proofs](BRIDGE_PROOFS.md) | Formal arguments with their assumptions; no requirement to start here |
| How can somebody check the result? | [Reproduction](ISC_REPRODUCTION.md) | Files, commands, controls and conditions needed for an honest comparison |

An old plan can say “the run is next” even though that run later finished.
That sentence records what was planned then. The status page provides the
current reading without changing the historical record.

## What the names mean

ISC-v5 and ISC-v6 are versions of the battery's rules. A change starts a new
measurement protocol; it does not silently change the verdict of an old run.

v25 is the carrier programme's name. It is not a count of battery checks.

The repository called
[aura-consciousness-proof-kernel](https://github.com/youngbryan97/aura-consciousness-proof-kernel)
contains an older component demonstration. It is separate from the current
24-check research programme. Its name does not describe a completed proof of
consciousness.

## Reading the numbers

A result needs a date, a source revision and a description of what actually
ran. “22/24” alone leaves out which rules were used and whether the whole
language model was included. The current status page supplies those details.

A small statistical probability is not the probability that Aura is conscious.
It belongs to a particular comparison under a particular test. Read it beside
the size of the change, the controls, and the stated scope.

For a presentation, the short explanation in [ISC explained](ISC_EXPLAINED.md)
is a starting point. Use the result box on the status page for current numbers
rather than reading an old work-log entry aloud as today's finding.
