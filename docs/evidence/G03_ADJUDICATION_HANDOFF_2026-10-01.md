# Development Adjudication Handoff, 2026-10-01

`tools/run_semantic_grounded_adjudication_handoff.py` waits for one already
running archived development evaluation, then verifies its terminal custody
and independently adjudicates the saved public programs. It cannot train,
load the backbone, rerun a decode or restart its prerequisite.

Before waiting it verifies the detached supervisor plan and the prospective
evaluation plan against their declared hashes. It takes the original parent,
source report, native artifact and every feature bundle from that exact
command. Missing, repeated or substituted inputs fail. Profile subsets and
evaluations without complete row archival are inadmissible.

The handoff freezes its own scripts, grading contract and arguments in a
governed immutable receipt. Waiting is bounded and checks the supported
detached status verifier once per minute. An indeterminate or dead supervisor
is rejected without another run. A terminal receipt must name the original
plan and command, show verified containment, have no timeout and leave its
child dead. Exit zero and exit two are admitted for custody checks; the latter
can describe a completed negative candidate.

After the prerequisite completes, the existing independent evaluation
verifier checks the fit, unchanged decoder, original population and every
public row. Its scientific verdict must match the original process exit.
Only then does the independent adjudicator load the original annotations
and regrade those rows. Completion retains both verification receipts,
metrics and the development verdict. A completed handoff with a negative
candidate is successful evidence processing, not semantic promotion.

The combined handoff, adjudication, native-evaluation and archive checks
passed 91 tests in 10.79 seconds. They cover terminal plan substitution,
timeouts, live children, changed inputs, unbounded waits, indeterminate
completion, target custody and the complete positive/negative workflow.
No model or decode occurs in those workflow fixtures.

completed five hundred requests each. Native replay had reached 291 without
a refusal. The handoff had not been launched and no completed full-cohort
score is claimed. G03 remains open.

Standard gates passed: smoke 164 passed and one skipped in 40.44 seconds;
lint, compile, governance, layering and writing passed without relaxed
