# G03: full-fold triadic replay reverses the pilot

The frozen fold-1 candidate that gained one exact program on a 40-source
slice was replayed on the entire 254-source held construction fold without
retuning. Exact programs were 214/254 for the cross-fit parent, 210/254 for
the candidate, and 214/254 for its triadic lesion. The candidate made two
parent-incorrect programs correct and six parent-correct programs incorrect.
Exact answers were 229, 226, and 229 respectively. All eight changed
programs were in the arithmetic constructions `fronted_obtained_afterward`
and `reverse_intermediate_before`. The immutable paired receipt is
`~/.aura/rlc-evidence/semantic-triadic-v4-oof-fold1-20260924/pair254.json`.
The 40-source gain was a misleading pilot. The candidate is rejected for
promotion; neither broad gain nor transfer has been established.

The inherited input-grounding contract was separately rebuilt from the
resident tokenizer with `semantic_input_grounding_contract_from_tokenizer`.
Its serialized value matched the inherited contract, with SHA-256
`1eb7949b96d6e1b370806c24be92bba2285b6db6ab1d35e4`. That contract is
compiled from tokenizer grammar, not fit on source semantic labels. Thus
the earlier phrase "inherited grounding" is not, by itself, evidence of
source-label leakage into this factor. The model's pretraining and persona
provenance remain separate; no end-to-end unseen-input claim follows.

The next score fit must optimize complete graph contrasts, including the
triadic score as a retained factor, on source-labeled fit data. A classifier
that distinguishes gold spans does not establish the marginal coefficient
that a full-program decoder needs. Selection and transfer must then be
measured on disjoint constructions, with no retuning on this exposed fold.
