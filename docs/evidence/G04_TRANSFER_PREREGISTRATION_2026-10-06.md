# G04 transfer: the plan, published before anything is decoded

G03 closed on 2026-10-06 with a frozen candidate, v12
([which and whether](G03_WHICH_AND_WHETHER_2026-10-06.md)). G04 asks whether
what it learned carries to requests it never met. This record publishes the
plan for that measurement. No fresh request has been decoded, and no feature
has been read from the model.

## What is compared

Two arms on the same tasks: the candidate (v12) and the incumbent it was built
on. The candidate's base is the incumbent itself, so the difference between
them is what G03 added: peak recognition, mention ownership, name antecedents
and the word readout.

## The fresh requests

Four strata of 62 requests, each differing from everything consumed in one
declared way (`core/learning/semantic_g04_transfer_corpus.py`, seed 20261006):

| Stratum | What is new | Checked against the consumed requests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| vocabulary | operation phrasings: "take 9 away from 46", "top up 46 with 4", "magnify the intermediate value 3-fold", "split 91 into 7 equal whole parts", "pluck the entry under selector 2 out of [...]", "find how many times 18 shows up in [...]" | none of 14 phrases occurs in any consumed request or generator table |
| construction | scaffolds: a question, numbered steps, a bullet list, a supposition, each naming the first result in its own words | none of 10 scaffold phrases occurs |
| depth | six and seven steps | consumed programs have two to five; seven is the most the candidate's geometry admits |
| family | a sequence operation taking a computed argument (a selector or wanted value worked out by arithmetic, or the count of an entry another lookup chose) | no consumed program has one |

"Consumed" is the whole population the candidate was fitted on, selected with
or tuned against, rebuilt from each feature bundle's own manifest by
`tools/build_g04_consumed_inventory.py`: 1,860 requests (train, exposed
validation, the sealed test split and both composition bundles). No fresh
request repeats a consumed text, task id or seed.

## Power, from evidence

The planned effect is not chosen. On the two composition bundles, the most
transfer-like evidence there is, v12 and the incumbent disagreed on 34 of 96
requests and v12 was right on all 34. Those bundles were exposed while G03 was
developed, so the plan uses the lower 95% bounds: a disagreement rate of
0.2592 (Clopper-Pearson) and a candidate win share of 0.9157 (one-sided, 34 of
34). With Bonferroni over the four strata (alpha 0.0125 each) and the exact
one-sided McNemar test, 62 tasks per stratum give power 0.9035 in each.

## Outcome and rules

* Primary: the decoded program means what the reference program means:
  `compare_program_meanings` over the public inputs and 32 counterfactual
  probes returns "equivalent". It reads neither model. "unknown",
  "different", a refusal and any runtime failure count as not equivalent, for
  both arms alike.
* Secondary: the executed answer on the public inputs; and whether each
  decode chose its program with program execution unavailable, which
  `tools/run_g04_transfer.py` enforces by making every executor raise during
  the decode. Execution runs only afterwards, for the answer.
* Fixed task count, no stopping early, no replacement of a task after
  interruption, arm order counterbalanced per stratum from order seed
  20261007, no change of candidate or threshold after publication.

G04's four comparisons all have to reject for the plan's claim to hold. A
stratum that does not reject is reported as it is.

## What this does not show

* Nothing about the result. The plan is published so the result cannot shape
  it.
* A strictly fresh population, in the sense of tasks nobody on the project has
  seen: the strata were generated by the person who will read the result, with
  one seed, once. What is fixed in advance is everything the result depends
  on.
* Transfer beyond these four kinds of difference. A fifth (new operations,
  new value types) would need a representation the candidate does not have,
  which is a question of information and grammar, not of transfer.
