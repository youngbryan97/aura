- **89/89** declared refutations hold. Each faculty states its own interventions as data — set self-agency to zero and guilt must collapse, make the loss recoverable and grief must fall — and the harness applies them and checks the direction.

The third is the one an emotion module usually cannot pass. A faculty that survives ablation without moving anything downstream is a description, and the gate names it rather than averaging it into a pass.

## What this does not show

The deltas are measured in the harness's activating world, not in a live conversation. They show that each mechanism reaches the consumers it claims, and that removing it is detectable. They do not show how often each fires in ordinary use, which needs the running instance and a period of it.
