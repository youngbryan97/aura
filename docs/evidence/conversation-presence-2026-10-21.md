# Conversation graphics and browser presence, 21 October 2026

Bryan supplied seven conversation screenshots and two Argon recordings. The
requested changes were useful graphics within a reply, visible work in a
background tab, and Aura's neuron glyph at her click target. He also asked
for a deliberate aesthetic pass on both surfaces.

The canonical desktop chat now adds charts to small numeric tables and metric
cards to single-row summaries. Unknown, approximate, mixed-unit and ratio
values retain their tables. Basic directed Mermaid flowcharts render locally;
unsupported syntax retains its code. Each graphic keeps its source available
in a disclosure. Stream completion and history use the same renderer. Narrow
conversation panes arrange flows vertically.

The browser uses the existing neuron artwork, a violet page outline, a target
highlight and a compact activity badge. Activity appears above the chat when
that panel is present. Its history contains action names and site origins,
without account paths, credentials, queries, field contents or page prose.
The mark sits in a closed shadow tree and accepts no pointer input. Capture
paths hide it while taking a picture. Expired marks cost no capture round trips.

The visual design uses Aura's existing dark and light palettes, tabular values,
fine borders, quiet gradients and restrained depth. The click pulse runs once;
there is no continuous decorative animation. Reduced motion is respected.
