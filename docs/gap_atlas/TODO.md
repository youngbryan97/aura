Do not edit this file; edit the adjudication and re-render. `make gap-atlas` fails on a card with no adjudication, a closed card whose test does not exist, and a closed card whose bar claims something about the whole system while naming only a module.

## The rules this list is kept under

**clean_room** — Every item is a CLEAN-ROOM adaptation. What is imported is the PROPERTY described in the public literature - an impasse is an occasion for learning, evidence must carry its lineage, a budget has priority and durability. No rival source is read, vendored, translated or paraphrased into this repo, and no rival identifier is adopted where it would carry a licence or imply provenance Aura does not have. Each module names the published description it was built from and states, in its own terms, what Aura does differently and why. This matches the discipline already in core/organism/model_validation.py (OpenWorm/sciunit) and core/cognition/impasse.py (Soar).

**gates** — make gap-atlas (every card adjudicated, every closed card's test and caller present), make architecture-lint (organ shape rules), make cognitive-complexity (the kernel ratchet, raising it requires a stated reason), plus the existing compile, smoke, lint, writing and layering.

**implement_to_what_aura_is** — The report prescribes rival-shaped mechanisms. Where Aura already has the property under another name, the work is to unify and prove, not to add a parallel organ. Where the rival's bar does not describe a non-human, non-embodied, single-machine actor, the entry is marked OVERSTATED and the transferable half is stated instead.

**section_level_bars** — The second review gives ONE acceptance test per section, so 193 of its 194 cards carry a bar that belongs to the section rather than to the card. Every adjudication here addresses the card's own subject - its title and what the rival actually demonstrated, recorded as card_subject - and answers the section bar separately. Five cards where the two point in opposite directions say so explicitly: A8.9 through A8.12 and A12.15, where the card's subject is a rival's deployment result and the section bar is a property Aura can and does implement.

**two_reports** — Two independent reviews landed on 2026-09-01: a comparative architecture atlas (220 cards, ids 001-220) and a cross-system engineering audit (194 items, ids A1.1-A13.15). They overlap heavily and disagree in places. Both are held here under one adjudication, and where an item appears twice the second entry names the first rather than duplicating the work.

**what_closed_means** — A card is closed when the bar it names is established by a test that runs, and - where the bar is a claim about the SYSTEM rather than about a module - by a production caller that imports the module. Neither of those is the same as the capability being demonstrated at scale. Nine campaigns are built and have not been run: the developmental grown-versus-reset campaign, the capability board against the bare cortex, the baseline portfolio, the environment bench, continual metrics, the retrieval latency law, world-model calibration, replication on other hardware, and the red-team trend. tools/evidence_report.py names each one and what it would establish, and the claim ladder holds four claims at CAUSAL and none at USEFUL, asserted by a test. The reviews' central finding was that the architecture is ahead of its evidence; this work builds the machinery that measures the gap and does not close it.
