## The rules this list is kept under

**clean_room** — Every item is a CLEAN-ROOM adaptation. What is imported is the PROPERTY described in the public literature - an impasse is an occasion for learning, evidence must carry its lineage, a budget has priority and durability. No rival source is read, vendored, translated or paraphrased into this repo, and no rival identifier is adopted where it would carry a licence or imply provenance Aura does not have. Each module names the published description it was built from and states, in its own terms, what Aura does differently and why. This matches the discipline already in core/organism/model_validation.py (OpenWorm/sciunit) and core/cognition/impasse.py (Soar).

**implement_to_what_aura_is** — The report prescribes rival-shaped mechanisms. Where Aura already has the property under another name, the work is to unify and prove, not to add a parallel organ. Where the rival's bar does not describe a non-human, non-embodied, single-machine actor, the entry is marked OVERSTATED and the transferable half is stated instead.

**two_reports** — Two independent reviews landed on 2026-09-01: a comparative architecture atlas (220 cards, ids 001-220) and a cross-system engineering audit (194 items, ids A1.1-A13.15). They overlap heavily and disagree in places. Both are held here under one adjudication, and where an item appears twice the second entry names the first rather than duplicating the work.
